## Story

James inherited Scotland as an infant and succeeded Elizabeth I in 1603. The Union of the Crowns placed England, Scotland, and Ireland under one monarch while their governments remained separate.

## Portrait provenance

Lifetime court portrait of James VI and I, traditionally dated circa 1610; the date inscribed on the work may not be its painting date.

The portrait is held by the National Maritime Museum, Royal Museums Greenwich, as BHC2796.
