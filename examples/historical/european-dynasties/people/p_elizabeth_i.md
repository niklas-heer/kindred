## Story

Elizabeth's forty-five-year reign ended the Tudor monarchs. Because she died without children, the crown passed to James VI of Scotland, a descendant of her aunt Margaret Tudor.

## Portrait provenance

The Darnley Portrait of Elizabeth I, circa 1575, probably painted from life.

The portrait is held by the National Portrait Gallery, London, as NPG 2082. Its artist attribution remains uncertain.
