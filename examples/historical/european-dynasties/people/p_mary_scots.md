## Story

Mary became queen when six days old. After a turbulent personal reign she was forced to abdicate in favour of her infant son James, later fled to England, and was executed after years in captivity.

## Portrait provenance

Traditional portrait of Mary, Queen of Scots; its broad circa 1560–1592 date means whether it was painted from life is uncertain.

Small oil-on-panel portrait held by the National Portrait Gallery, London, as NPG 1766.
