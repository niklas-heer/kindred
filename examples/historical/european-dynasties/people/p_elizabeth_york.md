## Story

Elizabeth's marriage to Henry VII joined the surviving Yorkist line to the new
Tudor dynasty. Four of their children represented here survived infancy:
Arthur, Margaret, Henry, and Mary.

## Portrait provenance

Posthumous portrait of Elizabeth of York, painted circa 1530, about 27 years after her death.

The white rose identifies the sitter as Elizabeth of York. The attribution is traditional; the artist is unknown.
