## Story

After years in Breton exile, Henry defeated Richard III at Bosworth in 1485. His marriage to Elizabeth of York joined the Tudor claim to the surviving Yorkist line and their children connected the English and Scottish crowns.

## Portrait provenance

Portrait of Henry VII painted during his lifetime on 29 October 1505.

The portrait was commissioned by Herman Rinck during negotiations over a proposed second marriage for Henry VII.
