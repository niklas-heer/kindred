## Story

Darnley and Mary, Queen of Scots were both descendants of Margaret Tudor. Their son James combined those two lines. Darnley was murdered at Kirk o' Field less than a year after James's birth.

## Research notes

Older accounts often give 1545 for his birth; the cited source reports that newer research supports 1546.
