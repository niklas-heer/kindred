## Story

Margaret worked for years to advance her only son Henry Tudor. After he became Henry VII, she used her wealth for religious and educational foundations, including Christ's College and St John's College at Cambridge.

## Research notes

The year of her birth is disputed; the source considers 1443 more likely than 1441.
