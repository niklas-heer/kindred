## Story

Henry became heir after Arthur's death. His pursuit of an annulment from Catherine of Aragon helped drive England's break with Rome; his three surviving legitimate children each later ruled.

## Portrait provenance

Lifetime portrait of Henry VIII by Hans Holbein the Younger, circa 1537.
