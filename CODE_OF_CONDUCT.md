# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is incorporated here by reference. Read it there; its text applies in full to everyone who takes part in Kindred's
repository, issues, pull requests and discussions.

In short: be kind, assume good faith, accept feedback gracefully, and do not harass or demean anyone. Kindred is about families,
and people bring their own, sometimes painful, stories. Treat what they share with care.

## Reporting and enforcement

To report behaviour that breaks the code, use one of these routes. Reports are handled privately.

- Open a private security advisory on the repository (**Security** tab, **Report a vulnerability**) and say it is a conduct report.
- Or contact the maintainer, [Niklas Heer](https://github.com/niklas-heer), through his GitHub profile.

The maintainer will review each report, may talk to the people involved, and follows the enforcement guidelines of the Covenant
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban). He will not share who reported.
