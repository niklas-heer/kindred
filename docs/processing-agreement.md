# Processing agreement (template)

> **This is a template, not legal advice.** It is a starting point for the agreement GDPR Art. 28(3) asks for between someone
> who runs a Kindred server for families (the *operator*, the processor) and each family that keeps its tree there (the
> *family*, represented by its stewards; the controller where the household exemption does not apply). Adapt it to your
> situation and your country, fill in every `[bracket]`, and have it checked by someone qualified before you rely on it.
> Kindred's own documentation of what the software does is in [gdpr.md](gdpr.md).
>
> To use it: copy the text below the line into the operator console (Settings → *Processing agreement*), edited. From then on
> whoever makes a family, or becomes a family's first steward, accepts it together with the server's terms, and the server
> records who accepted which version and when.

---

## Agreement on processing personal data on behalf of a family

**Between** the family that keeps its family tree on this server, represented by the steward who accepts this agreement
(*the family*), **and** [operator's name, address, contact email] (*the operator*).

### 1. Subject and duration

1.1 The operator runs the Kindred server at [address of the server] and keeps the family's tree there: its people, their
relationships, life events, stories, pictures, conversations and the family's settings (*the family's records*).

1.2 This agreement applies for as long as the family's records are kept on the server, and ends when they have been deleted
(clause 9).

### 2. Nature and purpose

2.1 The operator stores the family's records, shows them to the family's members and guests as the family decides, makes
backups, and runs the features the family switches on (for example the assistant, painted portraits, reminders, the nightly
archive).

2.2 The operator does not use the family's records for any purpose of their own. The operator's own processing (accounts,
sign-in, logs, statistics, the request forms) is described in the operator's privacy policy and is not covered here.

### 3. Personal data and people concerned

3.1 People: the people in the family's records (living and dead, members and relatives who are not members) and the family's
members.

3.2 Data: names, dates and places of life events, relationships, pictures, stories, contact details, and anything else the
family writes down. The family's records may include special categories of data (Art. 9 GDPR), such as health or religion.
The family decides what it records and is responsible for having a reason to record it.

### 4. Instructions

4.1 The operator processes the family's records only on the family's documented instructions. The family's use of the
software (what its stewards and members add, change, share, switch on or delete) is such an instruction. Other instructions
are given by email to [operator's contact email].

4.2 The operator tells the family without delay if they believe an instruction breaks data protection law.

### 5. Confidentiality

The operator ensures that everyone they allow to process the family's records is bound to confidentiality. The operator's
console shows how a family is run, never its records; the operator does not open a family's records except when the family
asks, or when the law requires it.

### 6. Security

6.1 The operator takes the technical and organisational measures of Art. 32 GDPR described in the server's record of
processing activities (downloadable from the operator console), including at least: sign-in by passkeys or comparably strong
methods, roles per family checked on the server, each family in its own database, encrypted transport, and backups.

6.2 [Describe your hosting, backups and how long backups are kept.]

### 7. Subprocessors

7.1 The family gives general authorisation for the subprocessors listed on the server's legal page (`/legal`, section
*Subprocessors*), which is generated from the server's configuration.

7.2 The operator tells the families of any intended change to that list [at least 30 days] in advance, by a notice in the
software or by email, so that a family can object. A family that objects may end this agreement and take its records with it.

7.3 The operator binds each subprocessor to obligations at least as protective as these.

### 8. Assistance

8.1 The software lets the family answer requests from the people in its records (access, correction, erasure, restriction):
see *A person in the tree* in [gdpr.md](gdpr.md). A request that reaches the operator is passed to the family's stewards
without delay.

8.2 The operator helps the family with security, breach notification and impact assessments (Art. 32 to 36) as far as the
operator's part allows.

8.3 The operator tells the family without undue delay, and within [48] hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach
that affects its records, with what the operator knows (the breach register in the operator console).

### 9. End of the agreement

9.1 When the agreement ends, the family may download its records (the family's export) and the operator deletes the family
from the server within [30] days.

9.2 Copies in backups are deleted as the backups roll over, within [period], and are not used in the meantime.

### 10. Proof and audits

The operator makes available the information needed to show that these obligations are met (the record of processing
activities, this agreement, the breach register's entries that concern the family) and allows reasonable checks, announced
[30] days in advance, at the family's cost.

### 11. Other terms

11.1 The law of [country] applies. [Courts.]

11.2 If a clause is invalid the rest remains valid.

[Place, date. The family accepts this agreement in the software; the server records the account, the version of this text and
the time.]
