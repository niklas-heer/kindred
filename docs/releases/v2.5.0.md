# Kindred 2.5.0

2.4 made Kindred something you can run for other families. 2.5 adds what running it for years needs: backups that prove
they work, a gentle end for families nobody visits any more, and email, so a forgotten password or an invitation no
longer depends on someone passing a link along. Try Kindred on two invented families first:
[kindred-demo.niklas-heer.workers.dev](https://kindred-demo.niklas-heer.workers.dev).

## For everyone

- **Email, if your server sends it.** Confirm your address under "Your email" in the account menu; then you can reset a
  forgotten password by email, and you hear about changes to how you sign in (a new passkey, a changed password). Emails
  come in your language, carry no tracking and link only to your Kindred. Notices can be unsubscribed with one click,
  except security notices, breach notices and a family's final notice before deletion.

## For stewards

- **Invitations by email**, with the link still there to copy.
- **A quiet family stays safe.** If your server's operator turns the lifecycle on and nobody visits for a long time, you are
  warned first; a read-only family comes back with one click, and you can always download a copy before anything is
  deleted.

## For operators

- **Backups** of every family and of the accounts, every night, encrypted, kept 7 days, 4 weeks and 12 months. A weekly
  restore drill proves one restores. The **Backups** tab restores a family into a new family (for the stewards its backup
  recorded; pictures only when they are all still its stewards) or over itself, and on Cloudflare rolls a family back to
  before an update. **Keep `KINDRED_BACKUP_KEY` (or `KINDRED_SECRET`) somewhere other than the server**: without the key a
  backup cannot be opened. `scripts/backup-open.ts` opens one offline.
- **Lifecycle** of quiet families, off by default: warning, read-only, deletion, with your thresholds, a dry run first and
  automatic deletion only after a final notice reached a steward.
- **Email** in the console's Email tab: Resend, Postmark, Mailgun (EU region) or SMTP on Node, with a test email and
  delivery problems on the Health tab. It needs `KINDRED_ORIGIN`, so every link in a message points at your server.
  Sending is limited per address, account, network and server, with a reserve for security mail.

## Upgrading

Back up first. Accounts migrations 0051 to 0057 are new, all adding tables or columns; on Node they apply at start, on
Cloudflare `mise run deploy` applies them before the new Worker goes live.

- Nothing changes until you turn it on: the lifecycle is off, email is off until a provider is set, and the backups begin
  with the next daily run (families on D1 bindings are covered by Cloudflare's own Time Travel instead).
- The Node build gains one dependency, nodemailer, for SMTP; the Worker does not include it.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
