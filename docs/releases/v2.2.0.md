# Kindred 2.2.0

2.1.0 gave the family a reason to come back. 2.2.0 makes that time better spent: a cookbook for the family's recipes, a
game you can play alone in a few minutes and compare with everyone, a feed that tells you what changed, emoji reactions,
seeing who of the family is here with you, a quick game of naming people in old photographs, portraits that can look like
you, quicker editing, and two tours that explain it all. Try it on two invented families before installing anything:
[kindred-demo.niklas-heer.workers.dev](https://kindred-demo.niklas-heer.workers.dev). The demo offers the grand tour first.

## What's new for families

- **Recipes and the Cookbook.** A recipe has a form of its own: ingredients in rows (type or paste "800 g beef, diced" and
  it sorts itself), numbered steps, servings, times, tags, where it comes from ("Grandma's") and a picture of the dish that
  stays with the recipe and is not in the Gallery. Its page scales the amounts, lets you tick off ingredients, has a
  cooking mode that keeps the screen awake, and prints as one sheet. The Cookbook gathers the family's recipes with search
  and filters. See [docs/recipes.md](../recipes.md).
- **The Campaign, played alone.** A reign is twelve turns as your own house on a map made from the family's places, about
  eight minutes. Each turn you place troops, then give up to three orders (choose a province, choose a target, confirm),
  with your exact chance shown before you attack. Forests, highlands and towns change the odds and the levy. Everyone
  plays the same field each week and compares scores in a weekly table, an all-time list and house totals; recording the
  family still raises your renown. The old shared seasons are kept as "Seasons of old". See [docs/campaign.md](../campaign.md).
- **Emoji reactions.** A quick row (❤️ 😂 😮 😢 🙏 👏 🕯️) and a palette with search in English, German and Afrikaans.
  The emoji data ships with Kindred; nothing is asked of an outside server. Existing reactions are converted.
- **Updates in the feed.** "Ada updated Carl: has a new address, new phone number": what changed, never the value, one entry
  per member and day. Changes only editors may read stay with editors. Members can react to and comment on these, and on
  birthdays and anniversaries.
- **Here now.** Faces in the top bar show who of the family is in Kindred with you and where; a soft halo marks the tree card
  someone else has selected, and the Edit tab says "Anna is editing Carl". When nobody else is there, it says who was last
  here. Members only, and "Show me to others" in the account menu turns it off both ways. See
  [docs/roles-and-privacy.md](../roles-and-privacy.md#here-now).
- **Who is this?** Untagged photographs become a two-minute game on Today, the Gallery and Needed. Anyone in the family can
  suggest who is in a picture; someone who may tag photographs confirms it, and the tag says who named them. See
  [docs/who-is-this.md](../who-is-this.md).
- **Portraits that can look like you.** Every hairstyle is redrawn as a shape of its own, with four new short cuts. Hair and
  beard take any colour, and a beard is chosen by coverage, length and cut, with the moustache separately. Existing drawn
  portraits change only in their hair and beard.
- **Quicker editing.** On the Edit tab each field folds to one line and only the one you are editing opens. The address box
  suggests whole addresses as you type. Search (⌘K, or the search icon on a phone) also finds places and ignores accents.
- **Two tours.** New members are offered **Welcome**, a short walk through what matters on the first day. **The grand tour**
  covers everything in chapters you can skip, pause and continue; some steps wait for you to try the thing, and "Show me"
  does it for you. Both are under Tours in the account menu. See [docs/tours.md](../tours.md).

## Upgrading

Back up first. This release adds six migrations:

- `migrations/tree` 0030 to 0034: emoji reactions and feed events (0030 makes the reaction and comment tables again with
  every row and turns each stored reaction into its emoji), recipes, the Campaign's reigns and its weekly field, and
  "Who is this?".
- `migrations/accounts` 0032: "here now".

On Node they are applied when the server starts, after a copy of each database. On Cloudflare, `mise run deploy` applies them
before the new Worker goes live. To go back to 2.1.0, restore those copies and start the old version.

Things to know:

- **Address suggestions go to komoot's Photon service.** The address box sends what is typed, through your server, to
  OpenStreetMap's Photon to suggest addresses. It is on by default; `KINDRED_ADDRESS_LOOKUP=off` turns it off, and the box
  then suggests only the family's own places and the offline towns. See [docs/roles-and-privacy.md](../roles-and-privacy.md).
- **The Campaign ranks reigns only with `KINDRED_SECRET` set** (every normal install has it). The old shared season, if one
  was running, is closed on the first visit after the upgrade; its nightly job and push are gone.
- **A new daily job, `recipes`,** deletes the pictures of removed recipes after 31 days.
- **Portraits:** only the hair and beard of drawn portraits change. A stored full beard with the moustache set to "none" now
  shows a shaven upper lip.
- Tour progress is kept in the browser only.

The full list is in the [changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md).
